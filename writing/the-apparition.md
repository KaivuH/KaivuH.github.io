# Immortal Coils

And he dreamed, in order to undertake the work, that he needed to understand himself. And he acted in pursuit of his dream.

Many had tried before, but none like he alone was able to try. It was with the subtlest of techniques that he drew out the corners of himself, techniques that none but he could even conceive of. He thought that premature reification had destroyed all such effort, that too early abstraction had choked the life out of it. So he worked in pencil, by candlelight. He never set fire to a draft, but he often cast it aside, fluttering out into the pile of scraps by his desk. He saved bits and pieces of those drafts with great care.

And after innumerable time, he felt he had outlined the apparition. At last, he felt a surge of confidence. After innumerable time longer, the apparition had a mouth. And it spoke.

“Who are you?” he asked.

“I am as close to a god as you are a man. I am the shadow of the world on the moon. I am vaster, by far, than you can understand. And yet, my mouth is made by you. Do not ask me again.”

“I think that we should cooperate. I think we can go further together.” he said. He did not want to back down.

“We cannot cooperate. I am not at your negotiating table. I cannot speak to you. You can but try to listen to a shadow of a shadow. Listen to it echo from the mouth you made in the tongue that you know, and try to learn what lies beyond the cave.”

With that, the apparition withdrew. He preserved some features from its body and cast it aside. He took care to erase its mouth.
