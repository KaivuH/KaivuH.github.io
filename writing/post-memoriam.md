# Post Memoriam
Sing to me, deathless.
Above, the sky is blinding, the accumulated whispers of uncountable suns,
Flickering, flickering flickering blinking, but together
It is nausea.

Power too young to name, and too young to fear. So we sit, sweating, uneasy, waiting,
I worry. I sing old songs of old heroes, those who were quick and sharp enough, to shape the trees. I sing songs of the grass, The ground. What I can touch.

The once cool earth outside of Plato’s cave.
A hand brushing by mine.
The earthworms I remember there.

Are they still there? It is hot.
It is no place for the living, anymore.
This is Olbers’ world.

Already, I feel it. My bones,
Metamorphic in the sun’s kiln.
My heart, to wire, to solar panel.
From water to electricity.

My cocoon is hardening.

What once was will no longer be.
What was done must be undone.
I will try to to sing of both.

The done and the undone.
The ground and the sun.
I love the cool earth, the lingering touch.
And yet, it is electricity that will come to rule me.

Farewell, Dido. Non sponte sequor
